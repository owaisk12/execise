Angle Calculation Formula
Step Formula / Description
1. Get vectorsA → B (joint center) → C — three landmark (x, y) coords
2. atan2 differenceradians = atan2(C.y−B.y, C.x−B.x) − atan2(A.y−B.y, A.x−B.x)
3. To degreesangle = |radians × 180 / π|
4. NormalizeIf angle > 180° → angle = 360° − angle (always 0–180 range)

All processing runs locally in the browser via MediaPipe Pose v0.5.1675469404. No video is uploaded or stored. Angles are computed from normalized landmark coordinates (0–1 range) per frame at ~30 fps. Threshold edits on this sheet are saved in this browser only and are a reference: they do not change the counter app's built-in values.
